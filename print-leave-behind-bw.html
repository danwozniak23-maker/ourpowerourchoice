How a Municipal Utility Works

City-Owned, Community-Run

The city acquires the local electric infrastructure. A local board governs it. Accountable to Cedar Rapids voters, not Wall Street.

Rate Increases

Alliant has filed multiple rate increase requests with the Iowa Utilities Board in recent years, with approved increases compounding costs for residential customers well above inflation.

Voters Decide

We want a 2027 ballot referendum. Cedar Rapids residents vote. Not the campaign, not the council alone.

The Morgan Valley Plant

Alliant says the proposed Morgan Valley gas plant is for local growth. The reality: new generating assets let Alliant grow its rate base, which is the mechanism it uses to justify raising rates on every customer it serves.
