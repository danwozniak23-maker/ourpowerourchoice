How a Municipal Utility Works

City-Owned, Community-Run

The city acquires the local electric infrastructure. A local board governs it — accountable to Cedar Rapids voters, not Wall Street.

No Shareholder Profit Margin

No dividend obligation means revenue goes to lower rates and reinvestment in our grid — not to investors in 49 other states.

Voters Decide

We want a 2027 ballot referendum. Cedar Rapids residents vote — not the campaign, not the council alone.

Proven Across Iowa

Ames Electric has run since 1890. Cedar Falls Utilities is among Iowa's lowest rates. This is not an experiment.
