Printing & Usage Notes

Postcard Stock

Print on 80–100 lb cardstock. Print front and back duplex, or print separately and glue back-to-back. Color printing required.

Leave-Behind (Color)

Print two copies on one 8.5" × 11" sheet, landscape. Cut in half horizontally. 60–80 lb text stock recommended.

Leave-Behind (B&W)

Any standard laser or inkjet printer. Plain 20 lb copy paper works fine. Print two-up landscape and cut in half.

QR Codes

Replace the QR placeholder boxes with an actual generated QR code pointing to ourpowerourchoice.com before final printing.

Scale Setting

In your browser print dialog, set scale to 100% (no scaling / actual size) to maintain correct physical dimensions.

Contact & URLs

Update email and website URLs in each file before printing if your final domain or contact info differs.
