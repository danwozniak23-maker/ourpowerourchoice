Sign the Petition

Add your name to the call for a Cedar Rapids municipal utility referendum on the 2027 ballot.

Why We're Using a Physical Petition

Iowa ballot initiative law requires verified signatures from registered Cedar Rapids voters. Physical signatures, tied to a real name, address, and voter registration, carry legal weight that an online form cannot replicate.

Each signature on this petition represents a real voter telling the Cedar Rapids City Council: we want this on the ballot. These petitions will be submitted as part of the formal record when we bring our case to the council and the Iowa Utilities Board.

We, the residents and registered voters of Cedar Rapids, Iowa, call on the Cedar Rapids City Council to place a municipal electric utility referendum on the November 2027 ballot, allowing Cedar Rapids voters to decide whether the city should acquire, own, and operate the electric distribution infrastructure currently operated by Alliant Energy (Iowa Power and Light).

Our Reasons Include:

  • Persistent rate increases with no local accountability
  • Shareholder profits prioritized over customer service
  • Stranded asset costs passed to ratepayers
  • No local voice in infrastructure or energy mix decisions
  • Proven success of Iowa municipal utilities in comparable cities

We believe Cedar Rapids residents deserve the same local control over their electric utility that residents of Ames, Cedar Falls, Iowa City, and Muscatine have long enjoyed. We are not asking the city to act unilaterally, we are asking for the right to vote.

How to Add Your Signature

We are collecting in-person signatures at community events, tabling locations, and through volunteers across Cedar Rapids neighborhoods. Find us, or bring the petition to us.

📍 Find a Signing Event or Volunteer

What Signing Looks Like

  1. Find a petition volunteer or event
    • Check the Get Involved page for upcoming tabling dates and neighborhood events
    • Volunteers carry petitions door-to-door in Cedar Rapids neighborhoods
  2. Confirm you are a registered Cedar Rapids voter
    • Iowa voter registration can be checked at mymvd.iowadot.gov
    • You must be registered at a Cedar Rapids address to sign
  3. Print your name, address, and sign
    • Legible printed name and current Cedar Rapids address required
    • Signatures are verified against Linn County voter rolls
  4. Spread the word
    • Tell neighbors, co-workers, and family, every Cedar Rapids voter counts
    • Ask your block captain or neighborhood association to host a signing
Our Goal: 5,000+ Signatures

Iowa ballot initiative thresholds and City Council petition requirements are still being researched and confirmed. We are targeting a strong showing of community support well above any legal minimum to make our case undeniable.
