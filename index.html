What Is a Municipal Utility?

A municipal electric utility is owned and operated by the city, meaning by the residents. Instead of profits flowing to out-of-state shareholders, revenue stays local to fund rate stability, infrastructure, and community priorities.

Cedar Falls Utilities, Ames Electric, and Muscatine Power & Water have all operated as munis for decades. Their customers consistently pay less and experience fewer prolonged outages than Alliant customers in Iowa.

This campaign is the first step: gather community support, compile the evidence, and get a municipal utility referendum on the 2027 Cedar Rapids ballot.

Why This Matters for Cedar Rapids

Lower Rates

  • Cedar Falls Utilities charges 44% less than Alliant, $695/year in savings for the average household
  • No shareholder dividend obligation built into your bill
  • Savings stay in Cedar Rapids

Better Reliability

  • Public power customers experience fewer outage minutes than investor-owned utility customers, nationally and in Iowa
  • Local control means faster storm response
  • Crews accountable to residents, not earnings calls

Local Accountability

  • Elected or appointed local board, not a distant corporate HQ
  • Rate decisions made in Cedar Rapids, not Madison, WI
  • You vote for the people who set your rates

Control Our Future

  • Set our own renewable energy goals, not Alliant's timeline
  • Decide locally whether to support data center load growth
  • Reinvest profits into grid modernization and efficiency programs

Alliant's Track Record in Iowa

These are facts on the public record, Iowa Utilities Board filings, rate cases, and state data.

💸 Rate Increases

Alliant has filed multiple rate increase requests with the Iowa Utilities Board in recent years, with approved increases compounding costs for residential customers well above inflation.

🏭 Stranded Asset Costs

Iowa customers are still paying $216M for the Sixth Street Generating Station that Alliant shut down in 2023, and will continue paying until 2037. You pay for their business decisions.

📊 Shareholder Obligation

Alliant Energy (NASDAQ: LNT) is legally obligated to maximize returns for shareholders. When your interests conflict with shareholder value, you already know who wins.

🏗️ Data Center Buildout

The proposed Morgan Valley gas plant is primarily intended to serve future data centers, not Cedar Rapids residents. But residential customers share the rate risk of that infrastructure.
