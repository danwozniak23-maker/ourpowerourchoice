Get Involved

This campaign runs on community volunteers. Here's how you can help, no matter how much time you have.

Ways to Help

Collect Petition Signatures

  • Go door-to-door in your neighborhood
  • Table at events and farmers markets
  • Host a signing at your home or workplace

Research & Data

  • Pull Iowa Utilities Board rate case filings
  • Compile EIA reliability data for Iowa utilities
  • Help with legal research on Iowa municipal utility law

Community Outreach

  • Present to neighborhood associations
  • Connect us to community organizations
  • Help translate materials for non-English speakers

Digital & Communications

  • Help maintain and improve this website
  • Create social media content
  • Write op-eds and letters to the editor

Professional Skills

  • Iowa utility attorneys, legal pathway analysis
  • Financial analysts, acquisition cost modeling
  • Engineers, infrastructure assessment

Financial Support

  • Fund printing of petition materials and flyers
  • Support yard signs and banners
  • Help cover event costs and legal fees

Contact Us / Sign Up

Fill out the form and tell us how you'd like to help. We'll follow up within a few days. No commitment required, even just expressing interest helps us understand our community's capacity.

Have a question? Use the same form, just describe your question in the message field.

Note: This site is currently offline / in development. Once the campaign goes live, this form will connect to a real submission system. For now, please note your interest and we'll set up real contact shortly.

✅ Thanks for reaching out! We'll be in touch soon.

Support the Campaign Financially

Every dollar goes toward petition printing, yard signs, event costs, and building the research case. No amount is too small, grassroots campaigns run on small contributions from a lot of people.

💚 How Donations Are Used

  • Petition sheets and clipboards
  • Yard signs and banners
  • Event tabling supplies
  • Flyers and door hangers
  • Legal consultation fees
  • Feasibility study costs
