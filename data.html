What This Means for Cedar Rapids Households:

Cedar Falls Utilities customers pay $695 less per year than Alliant customers for the same electricity. Across Cedar Rapids' ~60,000 residential accounts, matching Cedar Falls rates would mean roughly $41.7 million per year staying in Cedar Rapids rather than flowing to out-of-state shareholders.

Cedar Falls Utilities: Diamond RP3, Top 5% in the Nation

Cedar Falls Utilities earned the Diamond Reliable Public Power Provider (RP3) designation from the American Public Power Association in 2026, joining just 118 public power utilities nationwide and ranking in the top 5% for reliable and safe electric service. Cedar Falls does not just charge less. It outperforms investor-owned utilities on reliability too.

Alliant Does Not Report Reliability Data

Alliant Energy (IPL) has filed blank SAIDI and SAIFI data with the EIA every year from 2020 through 2024. Every other Iowa utility in this table reports annually. Alliant customers have no way to compare their outage experience against their neighbors, because Alliant won't publish it.

Sources

Alliant's Stranded Asset Cost Record in Iowa

When a utility builds or buys an asset and then retires it early, those costs are often passed to ratepayers as "stranded costs", you pay for their business decisions long after the plant stops running.

Sixth Street Generating Station

Alliant shut down the Sixth Street plant in Cedar Rapids in 2023 but continues to collect $216 million from Iowa customers through 2037. This was approved by the Iowa Utilities Board as part of a settlement in rate case proceedings.

The Morgan Valley Gas Plant

Alliant says the proposed 720 MW Morgan Valley Energy Center is reportedly needed for local growth. The reality: new generating assets allow Alliant to grow its rate base, which is the mechanism it uses to justify raising rates on every customer it serves. Infrastructure costs are socialized across all residential ratepayers, whether they benefit or not.

Iowa Municipal Utility Case Studies

These cities made the same decision Cedar Rapids is now considering, and have been running successfully for decades.

Cedar Falls Utilities

  • City-owned electric, gas, water, and broadband utility
  • Consistently among Iowa's lowest residential electric rates
  • Operates Cedar Falls Community TeleCom (broadband) alongside electric service
  • Profits reinvested locally, no out-of-state shareholders
  • Population comparable to Cedar Rapids neighborhoods

Ames Electric Services

  • City-owned since 1896, over 130 years of continuous municipal operation
  • Nationally recognized for reliability and low rates
  • Has led Iowa cities in renewable energy integration
  • Governed by Ames City Council, local accountability
  • Iowa State University purchases retail electricity from Ames Electric, making it a major anchor customer

Muscatine Power & Water

  • Frequently cited as Iowa's lowest residential electric rates
  • Electric utility established 1922, water utility 1900; merged 1929
  • Operates electric, water, and internet services
  • Revenue surplus used for community projects and infrastructure
  • Often used as the benchmark in Iowa rate comparisons

📚 National Context

There are approximately 2,000 municipal electric utilities operating in the United States. According to the American Public Power Association (APPA), public power customers on average pay lower rates than customers of investor-owned utilities.
