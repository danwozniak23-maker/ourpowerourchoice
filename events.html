Host an Event in Your Neighborhood

The fastest way to collect petition signatures is through neighbors talking to neighbors. If you're willing to host a small gathering, a backyard cookout, a living room meeting, a church basement, we'll send a volunteer with petition sheets, talking points, and materials.

Even 5–10 signatures from your block adds up. We need Cedar Rapids residents from every neighborhood, not just the ones we can reach at farmers markets.

Volunteer at an Event

Every tabling event and canvass needs volunteers. We provide everything, petition sheets, clipboards, talking points, and a brief orientation. You just need a few hours and a willingness to talk to your neighbors.

Tabling shifts are typically 2–3 hours. Canvasses run late afternoon to early evening. Town halls need people to help with setup, sign-in, and handouts.
